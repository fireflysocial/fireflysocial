Be generous to competitors; it reads as confidence. Event platforms own discovery and ticketing, but they’re built for hosts, and their job ends when you’re in the room. Friend apps start from profiles, not from somewhere you were already going. Curated outings like Timeleft come closest and even let you invite connections to a next booking, but they run their own small set of outings. Firefly sits on top of the whole city’s events and is built around the second meeting. The thing a neighbour can’t truthfully copy: turning registrations into repeat meetings with the same people, with consent at every step, and the trust that comes from never selling attention or data. Pods are next phase, not built yet. Why now, three forces. Technology: aggregating a city’s events used to mean platform APIs, and those closed (Eventbrite retired search in 2020; Luma’s API is calendar-scoped and Meetup’s needs Pro plus approval). AI web search finds events anywhere and extracts facts from each event’s own page in nightly batches per category and area, so cost stays flat and no personal data goes to the model. Demand: StatCan 2025 links in-person contact with lower loneliness (association, not causation), and recent immigrants are twice as likely to be virtual-only. Behaviour: Mondays Run Club draws 150+ a week; VanCity Girls Who Walk grew from 6 to 70–200. The crowd is there; nothing helps them find each other again. The table is a desk review, not an exhaustive feature audit.
