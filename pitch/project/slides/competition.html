Desk review of public product pages, 2026-11-15. Not an exhaustive feature audit.

Be generous to competitors; it reads as confidence. Event platforms own discovery and ticketing, but they’re built for hosts, and their job ends when you’re in the room. Friend apps start from profiles, not from somewhere you were already going. Curated outings like Timeleft come closest and even let you invite connections to a next booking, but they run their own small set of outings. Firefly sits on top of the whole city’s events and is built around the second meeting. The thing a neighbour can’t truthfully copy: turning registrations into repeat meetings with the same people, with consent at every step. Pods are next phase, not built yet.
