Design concept. Names and events are made up.

Dropbox’s pitch worked because the product was obvious in one look. Same here. Walk the phone: onboarding takes under two minutes and ends on the feed, so value comes first. Each card says why it’s there. “I’m going” is self-reported after you register on the event’s own page, because we never see other sites’ guest lists. Key point: Firefly is useful to one person on day one, before any network exists. That solves the cold-start problem every social app dies of. The feed is the launch product and is being built now; this screen is a design concept, not a shipped screenshot. Replace it with a real screenshot or a live demo when you have one.
