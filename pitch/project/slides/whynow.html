03 · Why now?

For the first time, software can read a whole city’s events.

AI CAN READ THE CITY

Every event, one pipeline

Models with web search find events on any site and read each event’s own page, in nightly batches. The old way is closed: Eventbrite retired search in 2020; Luma and Meetup gate theirs.

PEOPLE WANT IN PERSON

Screens aren’t enough

Canadians whose friendships are virtual-only: 24% often lonely, vs 15% with in-person contact. Recent immigrants are twice as likely to be virtual-only.

CLUBS ARE FILLING UP

People already show up

Mondays Run Club: 150+ a week. VanCity Girls Who Walk: from 6 people to 70–200. The crowd is there; nothing helps them find each other again.

Organizer API terms reviewed 2026-11-15. Statistics Canada, Survey on Social Connections, 2025. Vancouver Magazine, Feb 2025; Daily Hive, Jul 2024.

Three forces meet right now. One, technology: until recently, aggregating a city’s events meant platform APIs, and those closed (Eventbrite’s search API went away in 2020; Luma’s API is calendar-scoped and Meetup’s needs Pro plus approval). AI web search lets Firefly find events everywhere and extract facts from each event’s own page, run per category and neighbourhood, so cost stays flat and no personal data goes to the model. Two, demand: StatCan’s 2025 survey links in-person contact with lower loneliness (association, not causation). Three, behaviour: grassroots clubs in Vancouver are filling up, often started by newcomers themselves. The supply of events and people is there; the follow-through layer isn’t.
