02 · The problem

September 2026: I moved from Victoria to Vancouver for an eight-month co-op. I asked a friend who had lived here for two years how she builds real connections. She said:

“Once you find out, let me know.”

People here are polite, friendly, and busy. Conversations end with “we should hang out sometime”. Then nothing.

Airbnb opened with a human problem before any numbers; this is ours. Tell it in your own words, slowly. Then say the quiet part: this isn’t a lack of events or a lack of nice people. It’s the gap between a good conversation and seeing that person again. Note: “Vancouver Freeze” is a local expression, not a diagnosis; don’t claim Vancouver is uniquely lonely.
