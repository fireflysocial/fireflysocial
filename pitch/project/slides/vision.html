01 · The vision

Firefly turns a city’s events into people you keep meeting.

Friends, a professional network, collaborators, a co-founder: your people, found where you already go.

This is the one sentence to remember. Pause after it. The point: the events already exist; what’s missing is the part where the same people meet again. “Your people” is deliberately broad: some come for friendship, some for a network, some for a co-founder.
