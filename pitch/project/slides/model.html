06 · Business model

Free for people. Paid by organisations that want them to stay.

FOR PEOPLE

Free, always

The feed, pods and picks. No ads, no streaks, no selling data.

WHO PAYS

Institutions, [$__] per newcomer a year

Universities, co-op programs, employers who relocate staff and newcomer services, to help their people land.

Later: hosts and venues, for return-rate reports in aggregate.

Never for sale: your data, your going list, or your attention.

Proposed revenue lines, not yet validated with customers.

One pricing logic: the people using Firefly are free, and the organisations that want them to settle in and keep coming back pay. LinkedIn showed this can be venture-scale. First payer: institutions that already spend to help people land, such as a university for international students, a co-op office, an employer relocating staff, a settlement agency. They pay per person enrolled. Later, hosts and venues pay to turn first-timers into regulars, with reports in aggregate only: hosts never see picks or individual choices. An optional paid extra for attendees is possible later, but the feed, pods and picks stay free. What we won’t do: ads, selling data, or dark patterns like streaks and “someone liked you” upsells. In a product about meeting strangers, trust is the moat. All of this is a hypothesis to test with real institutions and organizers; fill the price once you have conversations behind it.
