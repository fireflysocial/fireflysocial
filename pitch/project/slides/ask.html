The ask, plainly: how much, what it buys, and the milestone it gets you to. Say the number once and stop. The money goes to three things: the Vancouver pilot (the event pipeline and personal feed), the social layer (pods and the set meeting point), and a safety, privacy and legal review before launch. The milestone that matters is the north star: people meeting again within 21 days. If that number moves in Vancouver, the same product opens the next cities. Fill the brackets before pitching, and tie the runway to the milestone, not to a burn rate.
