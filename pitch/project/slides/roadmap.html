04 · The solution

Useful alone on day one. Social as more people go.

LAUNCH

Your feed

Events across the city that fit you, found by AI and checked against each event’s own page.

NEXT

Your pod

3–5 people going to the same event, a set meeting point and a first question. No walking up to strangers cold.

THEN

Keep meeting

Private, mutual picks. The same people at the next event. Socials shared only by choice.

Casual friendship takes roughly 40–60 hours together (Hall, 2019). One event can’t do that; meeting again can.

This is the arc, and why it’s safe to start narrow. Launch is single-player: a personal feed. Every confirmed “I’m going” teaches Firefly who will be where, which is exactly what pods need next. Pods are 3–5 people because spontaneous conversation tends to cap around four (Dunbar, 1995), and assigned first contacts work: random seat neighbours at an intro session were more likely to be friends a year later (Back et al., 2008). Then picks: private, mutual, never revealed one-sided. Safety is built in, not bolted on: no location data ever, minimal profiles for strangers, one-tap block and report. Pods and picks are designed (v3 spec) but not built.
