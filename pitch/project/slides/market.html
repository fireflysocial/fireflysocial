Who needs this: newcomers first. Students, co-ops, new grads, people relocating for work, recent immigrants. Half of people in Canada under five years found it hard to make friends (Vancouver Foundation 2012), and recent immigrants are twice as likely to have only virtual contact with friends (StatCan 2025). Then everyone else looking for their people: friends, a network, collaborators, a co-founder. Fill the brackets bottom-up and show your maths: Metro Vancouver population aged 18–34 plus yearly arrivals (Statistics Canada census, IRCC data), times the share who go to events, times expected revenue per user from the business model slide. Investors trust a small, defensible bottom-up number more than a huge top-down one. The LinkedIn lesson: a network that starts in one tight community (newcomers in Vancouver) can expand city by city, and country by country, because each city is its own local network. Firefly is built to be multi-city and multi-country from day one; Vancouver is the first city, not the limit.
