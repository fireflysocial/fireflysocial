One Vancouver resident’s self-tracked year (Zetta H., Daily Hive, January 2025). One person’s account, not a population rate. Bars on a log scale.

This is the slide that frames the whole company. Zetta tracked a year of trying to make friends in Vancouver: roughly 2,000 opportunities, 305 conversations, 34 friends, and 2 people she still sees regularly. The leak isn’t at the top of the funnel. There are plenty of events and plenty of first conversations. It leaks at the second meeting. Every product in this space helps with the top; Firefly is built for the bottom. Be honest that it’s one person’s story, not a statistic. Her own takeaway: “Everybody is lonely, but no one wants to admit it.” (use only if it fits your tone; Firefly is never positioned as an app for lonely people).
