This slide frames the whole company. Zetta tracked a year of trying to make friends in Vancouver: roughly 2,000 chances to meet people, 305 people she talked to, 34 who became friends, and 2 she still sees regularly. The leak isn’t at the top. There are plenty of events and plenty of first conversations. It leaks at the second meeting, and every product in this space helps with the top. Be honest that the funnel is one person’s story, not a population rate. The 67% says she isn’t unusual: two in three 25–34-year-olds in Metro Vancouver find it hard to make new friends (Vancouver Foundation 2017); 60% of 18–24s say the same, and in 2012 half of people in Canada under five years did. If asked whether this is a Vancouver problem: no. Vancouver is where we measured it first; StatCan’s national data and urban-loneliness experts describe the same gap elsewhere, and Firefly is built for many cities across several countries.
