02 · The problem

Most young adults in Metro Vancouver find it hard to make new friends.

67%

of 25–34-year-olds find it difficult to make new friends

Vancouver Foundation, 2017

60%

of 18–24-year-olds say the same

Vancouver Foundation, 2017

50%

of people in Canada under five years found it hard to make friends

Vancouver Foundation, 2012

Connect & Engage (2017, n = 3,785) and Connections and Engagement (2012, n = 3,841), Metro Vancouver. Methods differ between waves.

Three numbers, one message: this isn’t a few people, it’s most young adults and half of recent arrivals. Be precise if asked: 67% and 60% are the 2017 survey’s age groups for difficulty making new friends; the 50% is the 2012 survey’s figure for people in Canada fewer than five years. These are regional surveys, not a ranking against other cities. The newest Vancouver Foundation wave is 2017; StatCan 2025 is on the next slides.
