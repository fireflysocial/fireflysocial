# Inspiration / Problem

In September 2026, I moved from Victoria to Vancouver for an eight month software engineering co-op. Moving to a much larger city, I assumed my social life would naturally expand. Instead, making genuine friends proved harder than ever.

When I asked a friend who has lived here for two years how she builds meaningful connections, she said: <b><i>"Once you find out, let me know."</i></b>

Her experience echoes accounts collected in our [desk-research synthesis](interviews/synthesis.md). The informal label "Vancouver Freeze" describes perceived difficulty forming connections; it is not a diagnosis or proof that Vancouver is uniquely lonely. The [Vancouver Foundation surveys](interviews/vancouver-foundation-surveys.md) document local friendship difficulties, while [expert commentary](interviews/douglas-college-ask-an-expert.md) notes that urban loneliness occurs elsewhere too. People here are often described as polite and pleasant in everyday interactions, yet reluctant to move from acquaintance to close friend. Some interviewees describe established high-school or university circles as difficult to join; we should not infer the motives of all long-time residents. Even when a connection seems promising, it often ends with a vague "we should hang out sometime" that never becomes an actual plan.

Interviewees also describe possible practical barriers. [Accounts of cost, work, weather, and travel](interviews/violet-myles-loneliest-city.md) suggest reasons plans can be difficult. These accounts do not establish which factor causes loneliness or how common each barrier is. For someone actively trying to make friends, every friendly conversation that goes nowhere wears down their motivation to try again.

Run clubs, events, and social apps already support connection, including repeat contact in some products (see [competitors](competitors/README.md)). Our hypothesis is that making follow-through easier at existing recurring events can help people who currently struggle with it.

Firefly Social proposes to bring together people who want platonic connections and help them meet repeatedly. Whether this produces lasting friendships is a question for the pilot, not an established outcome.
