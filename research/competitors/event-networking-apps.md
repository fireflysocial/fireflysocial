# Event Networking Apps (Brella, Swapcard, Grip)

**Category:** In-event matchmaking for conferences (B2B)
**Last researched:** 2026-11-15

## What they are

Conference apps that match attendees **inside a specific event** and book 1:1 meetings. They are the closest existing thing to Firefly's step 4 ("at the event"), built for professional networking rather than friendship.

## What they do

| Product | How matching works | Notes |
|---------|--------------------|-------|
| **Brella** | Questionnaire at login ("what I offer / what I want"). AI suggests matches, and attendees request and book meeting slots at numbered tables. | Organizer licenses per event. |
| **Swapcard** | Questions inserted into event registration. AI builds schedules and books thousands of meetings. | Vendor case study claims "nearly 100% attendee adoption, 3x more meetings". |
| **Grip** | Dating-app-style browsing: see who reached out, chat with mutual connections. | Started as a matchmaking add-on. |

## Strengths

- **Proves people will declare intent and book in-person meetings** if the event endorses it.
- **Organizer-paid.** The event buys the software, so attendees pay nothing. This is a proven B2B model.
- **Physical meeting points** (numbered tables, a meeting lounge) solve "how do we find each other."

## Gaps (relative to Firefly's goal)

1. **Value depends on adoption.** If only 50% of attendees engage, the other 50% gets nothing, and driving adoption falls on the organizer.
2. **Transactional.** "What do you offer / need" framing produces business meetings, not friendship.
3. **Ends with the event.** Contacts export to LinkedIn and nothing else follows.
4. **Enterprise pricing and setup.** Not usable for a 30-person run club or board-game night.

## What Firefly should take from it

- **Have the organizer bring it in.** Adoption is high only when the host pushes it during registration. That's the strongest argument for partnering with hosts over aggregating events.
- **Put the questions in registration.** Collect intent at RSVP time, not at the door.
- **Fixed meeting points** (a "Firefly table" or sign) beat live location for finding each other.
- A lightweight, friendship-focused, free-for-small-hosts version of this is an open niche.
