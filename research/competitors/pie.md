# Pie

**Category:** Social planning and events
**Reviewed:** 2026-11-15

Pie is Andy Dunn's social app. Its [official site](https://www.getpie.app/) describes creating plans for friends or communities, with chat, photos, RSVPs, tickets, and browser-based replies. The previous claim that Pie only owns its own events was incorrect.

## Supported facts

- Its counsel confirmed an **$11.5 million Series A in September 2024**, led by Forerunner. This is historical funding, not a current cash balance. [Wilson Sonsini](https://www.wsgr.com/en/insights/wilson-sonsini-advises-pie-on-dollar115-million-series-a.html)
- The [Creator Club](https://www.getpie.app/creator-club) rewards organizers. Current [points documentation](https://support.getpie.app/hc/en-us/articles/47508344990356-How-Points-Work) awards a point for each accepted or “Yes” RSVP; it does not establish a universal current US$5–10 payment per RSVP.
- A [creator dashboard](https://support.getpie.app/hc/en-us/articles/47510214302484-Creator-Performance-Dashboard) provides audience and plan analytics.

## Historical reporting and limits

[March 2025 coverage](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/04/andy-dunns-new-app-pie-uses-ai-to-help-you-make-friends/) discussed AI-assisted grouping. Earlier MAU, city coverage, payout rates, and Sparked rollout details require fresh verification before use as current facts. Canadian availability was not confirmed.

## Implications for Firefly

Pie competes on planning and organizer support. Test whether recurring pods add value beyond plans, guest lists, and group chat. No unit-economics conclusion follows from the existence of a creator fund alone.
