# Timeleft

**Category:** Planned meetups (dinner with strangers)
**Last researched:** 2026-11-15

## What it is

A Paris-founded app (first dinner 2023) that seats **six strangers at a restaurant every Wednesday**, matched by a personality quiz. It operates in Vancouver and other Canadian cities. Company figures vary across its own pages: 200 to 300+ cities, 52 to 60 countries.

## What it does

| Area | Details |
|------|---------|
| Format | Weekly dinner for 6 at a partner restaurant, then an optional "after-dinner" bar meetup where several tables mix. |
| Matching | Personality questionnaire. Algorithm assembles balanced tables. The restaurant is revealed in advance, and the tablemates are revealed on arrival. |
| Pre-event | Group chat opens 30 min before so people can find each other. |
| Post-event | Rate each tablemate. Historically, **mutual 4–5 ratings unlock a 1:1 chat**, and a 3 means "may be seated together again". A newer help article says everyone is now auto-connected. There are also "Impressions" (anonymous compliments). Group chat persists. |
| Price | Subscription (~CA$20–30 per dinner equivalent; monthly plans). Meal paid separately. |

## Strengths

- **Solves the first meeting completely.** No approaching, no awkwardness about who to talk to, because the table is the group.
- Small fixed tables are the right idea, though a table of 6 is above the ~4-person conversation cap and tends to split into two conversations (see [science.md](../science.md)). That is why Firefly Social uses pods of 3–5.
- Ships a **mutual post-event rating**, the exact double opt-in mechanic Firefly Social plans.
- Strong press and social buzz ("dinner with strangers" content).

## Gaps (relative to Firefly Social's goal)

1. **One-shot by default.** Each week is a new table. Seeing the same people again depends on the algorithm or on someone taking initiative in the chat, and most chats go quiet.
2. **Expensive.** Subscription plus a restaurant meal each week is costly in Vancouver.
3. **Fixed format.** Always dinner, always Wednesday. People who don't enjoy dinners, or who work evenings, are excluded.
4. **Matching quality complaints.** Trustpilot reviews mention mismatched age ranges, no-shows, and tables of people seeking dating or networking.
5. **Doesn't use existing events.** It creates its own events and doesn't help people at the hundreds of events already happening.

## What Firefly Social should take from it

- **Mutual post-event rating works** at scale and is socially acceptable. Validate the "who did you click with?" step with it.
- Pre-event group chat 30 minutes before is a cheap fix for "finding each other in the room."
- The biggest weakness is **no structured second meeting**. That's Firefly Social's step 6.
