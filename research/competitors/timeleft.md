# Timeleft

**Category:** Curated small-group social events
**Reviewed:** 2026-11-15

Timeleft matches people for social outings and arranges the venue. Its [current product description](https://timeleft.com/how-it-works/) includes dinners plus other formats, with availability varying by city. The previous description, “always dinner, always Wednesday,” was outdated.

| Area | Supported description |
|------|-----------------------|
| Matching | A personality quiz helps form groups. Group size and format can vary. |
| Coordination | [Group chat](https://help.timeleft.com/hc/en-150/articles/29192588664860-How-Do-Group-Chats-Work) opens 30 minutes beforehand and remains available afterwards. |
| Afterwards | Users can keep connections, chat, and invite connections to a subsequent booking. Repeat meetings are already a feature. |
| Price | Membership covers arrangements; food and drinks are paid separately. See the [FAQ](https://timeleft.com/faq/). A current Canadian price was not verified; the earlier CA$20–30 “per dinner equivalent” should not be used. |
| Safety | The company says blocked or reported people will not be placed in the same group again. |

## Implications for Firefly

Timeleft competes on repeat social contact, not merely first meetings. Firefly's proposed distinction is support for existing host-run recurring events. Whether that creates better outcomes remains untested.

Fixed meeting points and pre-event coordination are useful precedents. Small-conversation research suggests testing pod sizes, not assuming that every six-person dinner splits or fails.

## Verification limits

Older notes described mutual 4–5 ratings unlocking chat. Current documentation describes connections and invitations differently; do not present that historical rule as the current universal consent model. The old Timeleft Connect help URL returned 404. Claims that most chats go quiet or that no structured second meeting exists were removed as unsupported or outdated.
