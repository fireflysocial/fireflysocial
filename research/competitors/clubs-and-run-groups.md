# Clubs & Run Groups

**Category:** Offline recurring communities (run clubs, climbing gyms, rec leagues, board-game nights, choirs)
**Last researched:** 2026-11-15

## What it is

These aren't apps but the **real competitor for "where people make friends."** Run clubs became the social trend of 2024–2025. Strava's 2024 Year in Sport reported **running-club participation up 59% globally**, with **58% of participants making new friends** through fitness groups. Nearly 1 in 5 Gen Z had gone on a date with someone met through exercise. Vancouver has a dense run-club scene (brand-run clubs, coffee-shop clubs, neighbourhood clubs), mostly organized on **Instagram, Strava clubs, WhatsApp, and Luma**.

## What they do well

- **Repeated, scheduled contact.** Same people, same place, every week. This is the strongest predictor of friendship (see [science.md](../science.md)).
- A **shared activity** gives people something to talk about and makes approaching low-risk.
- Free or cheap, and they fit Vancouver's outdoor culture.
- Often end with coffee or beer, a built-in "third place."

## Gaps (relative to Firefly Social's goal)

1. **Hard to find.** Discovery runs on Instagram and word of mouth, with no central list.
2. **Walking in alone.** Big clubs (100+ runners) split by pace. Regulars cluster, and newcomers run alone and leave.
3. **Intent is invisible.** You can't tell who's there to make friends and who's there to train, so people assume nobody is looking.
4. **Dating-scene drift.** Coverage of run clubs as "the new dating apps" makes some people (especially women) wary of being approached.
5. **No bridge outside the club.** Club friendships can stay limited to the club ("run friends") and never move to other settings.

## What Firefly Social should take from it

- **Run clubs and other recurring clubs are Firefly Social's ideal launch partners.** They already have the repeated contact. Firefly Social adds visible intent, a newcomer buddy or pod, and a nudge to hang out outside the club.
- Firefly Social needs a clear **platonic stance** so it doesn't add to the run-club dating dynamic.
