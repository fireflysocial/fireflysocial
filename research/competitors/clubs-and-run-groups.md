# Clubs & Run Groups

**Category:** Offline recurring communities (run clubs, climbing gyms, rec leagues, board-game nights, choirs)
**Last researched:** 2026-11-15

## What it is

These aren't apps but the **real competitor for "where people make friends."** Run clubs became the social trend of 2024–2025. Strava's 2024 Year in Sport reported **running-club participation up 59% globally**, with **58% of survey respondents reporting new friends** through fitness groups. These are Strava's activity data and survey results, not a representative estimate for all Vancouver residents. [Official release](https://press.strava.com/articles/strava-releases-annual-year-in-sport-trend) Nearly 1 in 5 Gen Z had gone on a date with someone met through exercise. Vancouver has a dense run-club scene (brand-run clubs, coffee-shop clubs, neighbourhood clubs), mostly organized on **Instagram, Strava clubs, WhatsApp, and Luma**.

## What they do well

- **Repeated, scheduled contact.** Same people, same place, every week. This is consistent with research on proximity and repeated contact (see [science.md](../science.md)).
- A **shared activity** gives people something to talk about and makes approaching low-risk.
- Free or cheap, and they fit Vancouver's outdoor culture.
- Often end with coffee or beer, a built-in "third place."

## Gaps (relative to Firefly's goal)

1. **Hard to find.** Discovery runs on Instagram and word of mouth, across multiple directories and channels; this audit did not establish that no directory exists.
2. **Walking in alone.** Large clubs may split by pace. Whether newcomers struggle to connect depends on host practices and should be observed locally.
3. **Intent is invisible.** You can't tell who's there to make friends and who's there to train, which can make intent ambiguous; some clubs explicitly welcome newcomers.
4. **Dating-scene drift.** Coverage of run clubs as "the new dating apps" makes some people (especially women) wary of being approached.
5. **No bridge outside the club.** Club friendships can stay limited to the club ("run friends") and never move to other settings.

## What Firefly should take from it

- **Run clubs and other recurring clubs are Firefly's ideal launch partners.** They already have the repeated contact. Firefly adds visible intent, a newcomer buddy or pod, and a nudge to hang out outside the club.
- Firefly needs a clear **platonic stance** so it doesn't add to the run-club dating dynamic.
