# 222

**Category:** Curated social outings
**Reviewed:** 2026-11-15

The [official site](https://222.place/) advertises planning experiences with friends and new matches, including coffee and board games. The earlier claim that every outing is necessarily a new group and that deepening relationships is not a goal was too strong.

## Evidence and limits

[TechCrunch's 2022 profile](https://techcrunch.com/2022/11/14/2440702/) and [AFP coverage from September 2025](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2025/09/08/ai-powered-meet-up-apps-fight-loneliness) provide historical context.

Earlier notes gave a 14-city footprint, US$22 monthly price, 16 personality categories, sensitive onboarding questions, and a 50/50 friendship/romance split. Those details were not established as current in this audit and should not guide a current comparison. Vancouver availability is **unverified**, not a confirmed absence.

## Implications for Firefly

Curated outings and deeper relationships already have competitors. Test a clearly platonic, minimal-data approach on existing recurring events. Whether users prefer it or feedback improves pod matching remains a hypothesis.
