# Facebook Events & Groups

**Category:** Events and communities
**Reviewed:** 2026-11-15

Meta's [October 2024 announcement](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/10/facebook-local-tab-messenger-communities-ai/) described tests of a Local tab combining nearby groups, events, and Marketplace content. It also reported 25 million monthly active public groups. This is a dated company figure, not a current independent audience measurement or proof of Vancouver availability for every tested feature.

## Relevant capabilities

Facebook supports public and private events, interest groups, and invitations. Groups can explicitly focus on friendship or newcomers; therefore, saying intent is entirely absent is too broad. The reviewed announcement does not establish a Firefly-style checked-in pod and mutual re-meeting workflow.

## Implications for Firefly

Local groups may be useful research and recruitment channels. Validate willingness to participate and moderator rules before outreach. Public visibility is not consent to republish member data.

Brand fatigue, low post-to-meeting conversion, and migration to other apps are interview hypotheses here. No representative age-specific preference or conversion study was verified to support the earlier broad claims.
