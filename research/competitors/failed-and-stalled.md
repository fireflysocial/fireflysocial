## Hypotheses to test

Retention, follow-through, host costs, and willingness to pay are useful questions. These cases do not establish that chat-first loses to event-first, every matching app pivots to groups, or charging organizers necessarily fails.

Successful users may leave or continue using planning tools; measure both. [The Next Web's March 2026 discussion](https://thenextweb.com/news/friending-app-loneliness-real-world-friendships) is industry commentary, not a representative retention benchmark.
