## The funnel (her 2024 data)

| Stage | Count |
|---|---|
| Had the chance to meet | ~2,000 |
| Actually talked to | 305 |
| "Friends" (added on socials / see in group settings) | 34 |
| Talk to regularly | **2** |

## Quotes

> "My biggest takeaway is that everybody is lonely, but no one wants to admit it." — **Zetta H.**

> "Social meetups are a good way to meet a lot of people at once, but you really have to hit it off with someone…" — **Zetta H.**

> "I think making friends in Vancouver is hard because we all have so much on our plates." — **Zetta H.**

> "If you have chemistry, you have chemistry, and it doesn't matter how you met." — **Zetta H.**

> "A friend is someone who wants to struggle through life together." — **Zetta H.**

> Asked what made her two closest friends stand out: "They bought me fried chicken." — **Zetta H.**

> "I made only two attempts last year, got ghosted both times." — **Reddit commenter** (goes on to say it's less painful not to try)

> "This tracks. It is hard because not everyone has the longevity when it comes to follow through." — **Reddit commenter**

## Takeaways

- The best quantitative picture in this folder of the **acquaintance → friend drop-off**: 305 conversations became 2 real friends (<1%).
- The bottleneck isn't meeting people, it's **follow-through**. Commenters name ghosting and lack of "longevity."
- Being ghosted makes people **stop trying**.
- Her two friends stood out because of a small act of **reciprocal effort**.
