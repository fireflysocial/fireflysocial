## Recurring themes

Ranked by how many independent sources raise each one.

1. **Meeting people is easy; the second meeting is the problem** (CBC, Zetta, Zada/Beattie, Reddit, We Should Be Friends, Lumi). Zetta's funnel (305 conversations → 2 friends) is the clearest evidence. People swap numbers, then nobody follows up: *"People don't call you back, people don't invite you out."*
2. **Closed circles and a "leave me alone" norm** (CBC/Sofia Katrina, BCIT/Lee and Wong, Myles/Juliette, Zada, Narcity). Locals already have friends from high school or university. People are polite but don't *invite* others in: *"'I'll leave you alone—if you leave me alone' is the overall vibe."*
3. **Time, money, and exhaustion** (Vancouver Foundation, BCIT, Namoro/Esme, Myles/Daniel, Zetta). $15–20 per hangout, paycheque-to-paycheque living, 6–7 night workweeks. Time alone more than wanted rises from 23% of all residents to 47% of households under $20K.
4. **Reaching out feels risky** (Anna Ho: "like a job interview," Maryam: fear of looking like a "tryhard," Reddit: "got ghosted both times"). Rejection makes people stop trying.
5. **Recurring, free, low-commitment formats are what work** (Girls Who Walk, Mondays Run Club, book club → "auntie to a baby," Lumi's "little by little," UBC roommates). Every success story involves seeing **the same people repeatedly**.
6. **Shared intent removes the awkwardness** (We Should Be Friends: "everyone else here came alone too," Namoro: "everyone is going with the same intention," Reddit: wary of strangers "until they ask for something").
7. **Newcomers are hit hardest, but it isn't only them.** 50% of people in Canada for under five years find it hard to make friends, and recent immigrants' friendships are twice as likely to be online-only (StatCan 2025). Lifelong locals (Anna Ho, Esme) and long-time residents ("lived here for years and still had no friends") feel it too.
8. **Founders are former lonely newcomers.** Lumi, We Should Be Friends, Mondays Run Club, and VanCity Girls Who Walk were all started by people who moved here and couldn't find friends. They are natural host partners (now listed in [organizers.md](../organizers.md)).

**Counter-evidence (keep it honest):** some people say it's a myth if you put in effort (Narcity OP, Dylan Wharndy: "I'll meet friends at the bar"). A friendship coach warns the "freeze" label can become an excuse. Dr. Joey Moore (Douglas College) says it's "not just a Vancouver problem." Cluny South (KPU) notes that cities with a lot of newcomer flux tend to be *more* open. The 2017 survey found loneliness "lower than expected" (14% often or almost always), even though about half find it hard to make friends.

## Implications for the workflow

Mapped to [v1-user-flow.md](../../workflow/v1-user-flow.md) (v2 draft):

| Finding | Flow step | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| Follow-through is the bottleneck (theme 1) | **6 Second meeting** | Strong support for step 6. The "same time next week" default matches Martin's "plan within two weeks" rule. Consider tightening the **21-day fallback** (no shared event) to **14 days**. |
| Ghosting makes people quit (theme 4) | 5 After, 7 Ongoing | Keep non-mutual picks invisible (already planned). Also nudge the person who **hasn't replied** in a new chat, so the other person never experiences silence as rejection. |
| Closed circles (theme 2) | 3 Before (pods) | Pods give newcomers a ready-made circle instead of asking them to break into one. At first meetings, weight pods toward **similar newness**. |
| Fear of looking like a "tryhard" (theme 4) | 0 Onboarding, 4 At event | Visible, shared intent is the answer. In the host's 60-second intro, say **"everyone here came alone too."** |
| Cost (theme 3) | 0 budget preference, 1 Discover | Keep the budget filter and **rank free events first** for "free" users ([open-gaps #16](../../workflow/open-gaps.md)). |
| Recurring formats work (theme 5) | 1 Discover | Supports "recurring series ranked first." Big clubs (150–200 people) are great supply but need pods so people **find the same people again**. That is Firefly's pitch to hosts ([open-gaps #9](../../workflow/open-gaps.md)). |
| In-person contact is linked to lower loneliness (StatCan 2025) | Overall | Supports in-person-first over chat-first ([failed-and-stalled](../competitors/failed-and-stalled.md)). Keep chat as a means to the next meeting, not the product. |
| Newcomers hardest hit (theme 7) | 0 Onboarding, [open-gaps #37](../../workflow/open-gaps.md) | Supports targeting newcomers in their first 3 months. But don't position Firefly as newcomers-only, because locals whose friends moved away are users too. |
| Founders were lonely newcomers (theme 8) | H Host flow | First outreach list: Lumi, We Should Be Friends, Mondays Run Club, VanCity Girls Who Walk, Let's Adventure Vancouver ([organizers.md](../organizers.md)). |
