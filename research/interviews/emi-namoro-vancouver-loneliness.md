> "Why not create a group where I bring people together and they can create their own groups…" — **Sebastian Breuninger**, via CBC (truncated)

> "When we don't exercise our social skills, our attitude about social connection changes." — **Kimberley Brownlee**, UBC, via CHEK

## Takeaways

- **Money and exhaustion** come up again: living paycheque to paycheque leaves no energy for socializing.
- **Shared intent** is what makes meet-ups work. Everyone is there to make friends, so reaching out isn't awkward.
- Loneliness compounds: the less people socialize, the less they want to (Brownlee).
