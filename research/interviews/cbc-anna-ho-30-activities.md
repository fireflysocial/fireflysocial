## Numbers

- Ho did at least one activity a week; activities cost **"hundreds of dollars so far."**
- Less than a third of the way through the 30 activities, she had made **one friend**.
- InterNations ranked Vancouver among the **10 worst of 50 cities** for expats.
- The City of Vancouver launched a task force on isolation in 2012.

## Takeaways

- Even a **lifelong local** says she has no friends. The freeze isn't only a newcomer problem.
- Meeting people feels like **work** ("job interview," "dates"), so effort drains motivation fast.
- Closed circles: people are friendly but don't **invite** newcomers into the inner group.
- More activities don't mean more friends: fewer than 10 activities in, she had one friend and had spent hundreds of dollars.
