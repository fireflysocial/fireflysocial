## Quotes

> "If I had money to blow, I wouldn't be alone for one second." — **Daniel**, bar back from Calgary

> "I like to be the one that's included, but I don't always feel like I need to do the same for others." — **Juliette**, 22, Vancouver-born. She describes a **"stay in your lane"** culture and calls the city **"hyper-segmented."**

> "if a stranger strikes up a conversation with me, I'm just counting down the seconds until they ask for something" — **Reddit poster**

> "Meaningful friendships don't just fall out of the sky into your lap, they take work…" — **Reddit skeptic** (truncated)

## Numbers

- 2012 Vancouver Foundation: **1/3** say it's hard to make friends here; **1/4** are alone more than they'd like. Most people know two neighbours by name, "the connections typically stop there."
- The author's transit times from campus: 30 min to a friend's, 50 min to Main St, 1 h to Commercial Dr.

## Takeaways

- The most candid **insider** quote in the folder: a local admits she likes being included but doesn't feel she has to include others. That is the freeze, in her own words.
- **Cost and work schedules** keep people home (Daniel).
- **Distance and transit time** make casual hangouts a commitment.
- People are **suspicious** of friendly strangers ("until they ask for something"), so a context with clear platonic intent matters.
