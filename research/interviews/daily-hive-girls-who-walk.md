## Numbers

- Started in 2023 with ~6 people; now **70–80** on average, sometimes **120–200** (founder's estimate).
- Free.

## Takeaways

- **Free + low-commitment + weekly** is the formula people praise ("easy to just show up").
- The "church group" framing: a **recurring ritual** is what turns attendance into belonging.
- Loneliness affects long-time residents too, not only newcomers.
- Note: groups of 100+ are great for showing up but make it hard to find the same people again.
