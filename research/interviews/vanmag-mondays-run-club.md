## Quotes

> "I decided before I even moved to Vancouver: I'm going to be one of those cool girls who runs the seawall." — **Veronica Skye**

> "We ran, took breaks, grabbed a beer—and it was really fun." — **Veronica Skye**

> "There were like 40 people, and we thought, 'OK, this is weird'… But then I realized it was likely New Year's resolutions." — **Veronica Skye**

> "All you have to do is show up. That's where the magic happens." — **Veronica Skye**

## Numbers

- Year one: a steady **~20** regulars. Now **150+** runners weekly, a seven-person leadership team, free, and "they've yet to miss a Monday."

## Takeaways

- Another founder who started a club **because she was a lonely newcomer**.
- The social part (beer after) matters more than the run.
- Consistency (never missing a Monday) is what built the community.
- At 150 people, the challenge shifts from "is anyone there?" to "how do I find the same people again?"
